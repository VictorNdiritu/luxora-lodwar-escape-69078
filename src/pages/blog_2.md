# Exploring Turkana: Places to Visit and Things to Do in Northern Kenya

Turkana offers a side of Kenya that many travellers have yet to experience. Its landscapes stretch from the shores of Lake Turkana to volcanic hills, open plains and remote archaeological sites. Its communities have long adapted to an arid environment, developing distinctive traditions, livelihoods and relationships with the land.

Known as the Jade Sea, Lake Turkana is the defining feature of the region. Its unusual colour, geological formations and importance to wildlife make it one of the most striking destinations in the country. Beyond the lake, visitors can explore cultural heritage, fossil sites, local markets and landscapes that feel far removed from Kenya's busier tourist circuits.

The challenge is deciding where to begin. Turkana County is large, and the distances between attractions mean that a rewarding visit depends on choosing a realistic itinerary.

Whether you're planning a short stay in Lodwar or a longer expedition across northern Kenya, here are some destinations and experiences worth considering.

## 1. Lake Turkana: The Jade Sea

Lake Turkana is one of the most remarkable natural features in Kenya. Its blue-green waters contrast sharply with the surrounding arid landscape, creating scenery that changes with the light throughout the day.

It is the world's largest permanent desert lake and forms part of the Lake Turkana National Parks UNESCO World Heritage Site. The area is important for its geological features, wildlife habitats and archaeological discoveries.

### What to do

Spend time observing the lake from suitable viewpoints, particularly in the early morning or late afternoon when the light can be excellent for photography.

Depending on where you visit, you may also be able to arrange a locally guided shoreline excursion or learn about fishing communities that depend on the lake.

Birdwatching is another worthwhile activity. The lake and its surrounding habitats support numerous bird species, including migratory waterbirds.

Lake Turkana is not simply a place to admire from a distance. It is an important source of livelihood and part of the cultural identity of communities around its shores. Learning about this relationship can make your visit more meaningful.

**Travel tip:** Do not assume that the lake is safe for swimming. Nile crocodiles and other potentially dangerous wildlife inhabit its waters. Follow local safety guidance and use qualified operators for any water-based excursion.

## 2. Eliye Springs: A Different Pace of Travel

Eliye Springs, on the western shore of Lake Turkana, is a destination for travellers who want to experience the lake at a slower pace.

Its sandy shoreline, open views and lakeside atmosphere make it a good option for photography, relaxation and time outdoors. Compared with an expedition into a remote national park, a visit here can be relatively straightforward to arrange from Lodwar, although transport and road conditions should still be confirmed.

### What to do

- Enjoy the lakeside scenery.
- Photograph the water and surrounding landscape.
- Watch the changing colours of the sky at sunset.
- Arrange a guided walk or local excursion where available.
- Learn about the area's fishing traditions.
- Spend time relaxing at a suitable lakeside accommodation facility.

The best experience may simply be allowing yourself time to enjoy the surroundings rather than rushing between activities.

Before visiting, confirm your transport, meals and accommodation arrangements. Facilities outside the main town are more limited, and you should not assume that supplies will be available at every stop.

## 3. Lodwar: The Gateway to Turkana

Lodwar is the administrative headquarters of Turkana County and a practical starting point for exploring the region.

For first-time visitors, it provides an opportunity to settle in, organise transport and obtain supplies before heading towards the lake or more remote attractions.

### What to explore

Take time to experience the town itself. Visit local markets and shops, sample locally available food and learn about everyday life in Turkana's main urban centre.

Lodwar is also useful for arranging onward journeys. Depending on your interests, you can organise a trip towards Eliye Springs, explore the western shore of Lake Turkana or plan a longer excursion with an experienced local operator.

If you're arriving by air, consider spending a night in town before beginning a long journey. It gives you time to rest, confirm your arrangements and prepare for the distances involved.

Luxora Hotel Lodwar, situated adjacent to Lodwar Airport, is one accommodation option for travellers staying in the town. Its location can be convenient for visitors coordinating airport transfers and onward transport.

Rather than treating Lodwar merely as a stopover, use it as a place to understand the region and prepare for the experiences ahead.

## 4. Kalokol: Discover the Fishing Communities of Lake Turkana

Kalokol is a fishing town on the western shore of Lake Turkana. Fishing and the movement of fish products are important parts of the local economy, making the area interesting for visitors who want to understand the lake beyond its scenery.

### What to do

Observe fishing-related activities from appropriate public areas, learn about local livelihoods through respectful conversations and ask a local guide about opportunities to visit the lakeshore.

Depending on current arrangements and conditions, Kalokol can also be a starting point for certain boat excursions, including trips towards Central Island National Park.

Photography can be rewarding, particularly when the light falls across the lake and boats. Always ask permission before photographing people or private activities.

If you are arranging a boat trip, confirm the route, safety arrangements, weather conditions and expected return time in advance.

## 5. Central Island National Park: Volcanic Landscapes

Central Island National Park is one of the most distinctive destinations on Lake Turkana. Its volcanic landscape features three crater lakes known as Crocodile, Flamingo and Tilapia.

The island is also significant for its birdlife and Nile crocodile breeding habitat.

### What to do

Explore the island's unusual geology, observe birds from a safe distance and photograph its rugged terrain. A knowledgeable guide can help explain the volcanic formations and ecological significance of the crater lakes.

Getting there requires advance planning. Boat arrangements, weather conditions, park requirements and the availability of experienced operators all need to be considered.

This is not an excursion to organise casually on arrival. Confirm your transport and safety arrangements before leaving Lodwar or Kalokol, and carry sufficient drinking water and other essentials.

Central Island is particularly appealing to travellers interested in geology, wildlife and photography.

## 6. Loiyangalani: Cultural Heritage and Desert Landscapes

Loiyangalani lies on the southeastern shore of Lake Turkana in neighbouring Marsabit County. The area combines desert scenery, lakeside views and cultural experiences associated with communities across the wider region.

Because it is distant from Lodwar, Loiyangalani is best approached as part of a separately planned journey rather than a quick day trip.

### What to do

Explore the town with a local guide, learn about the communities that live around the lake and visit the Loiyangalani Desert Museum.

You can also ask about local crafts, cultural events and community-led experiences that offer insight into life in this part of northern Kenya.

The region is culturally diverse, and different communities have their own traditions, livelihoods and histories. Approaching these experiences with curiosity and respect is important.

Before departing, confirm accommodation, transport, fuel availability and the current condition of your intended route.

## 7. The Loiyangalani Desert Museum

For travellers interested in the human side of the region, the Loiyangalani Desert Museum offers an introduction to the cultural and environmental heritage of the Lake Turkana area.

It can provide useful context for understanding the relationship between the desert, the lake and the communities that live around them.

Allow time to explore the exhibits and ask questions where appropriate. The museum can also help visitors appreciate the region's cultural diversity before undertaking further exploration.

Check current opening arrangements before making a special journey to visit.

## 8. Sibiloi National Park and Koobi Fora

Sibiloi National Park, on the eastern shore of Lake Turkana, is one of Kenya's most important destinations for archaeology and human evolution.

The Koobi Fora region has produced significant fossil discoveries that have helped scientists understand early human ancestry and the environments in which ancient species lived.

### What to do

- Explore the park's desert landscapes.
- Learn about the region's archaeological significance.
- Visit relevant sites with appropriate guidance and arrangements.
- Observe wildlife and birdlife.
- Photograph the dramatic scenery.
- Learn about the geology and ancient environments of the area.

This destination is particularly rewarding for travellers interested in science, history and the origins of humanity.

However, reaching Sibiloi requires careful planning. Kenya Wildlife Service advises that a high-clearance 4x4 vehicle is essential and recommends travelling in convoy.

Confirm access, park requirements, guides, accommodation and transport well in advance. The journey should be treated as a dedicated expedition rather than an add-on to a short visit to Lodwar.

## 9. South Island National Park

South Island National Park is another protected area within the Lake Turkana National Parks World Heritage Site.

Its volcanic terrain, rocky formations and shoreline habitats offer further opportunities to appreciate the lake's unusual geology and ecology.

Visitors interested in birdwatching, natural history and remote landscapes may find the island particularly appealing.

As with other island destinations, reaching it requires advance transport arrangements and careful consideration of conditions on the lake.

Confirm the availability of suitable local operators and any necessary permissions before including it in your itinerary.

## 10. Cultural Experiences Across Turkana

A trip to Turkana is incomplete if it focuses entirely on scenery. The region's communities are an important part of what makes it distinctive.

Turkana communities have a rich cultural heritage shaped by pastoralism, craftsmanship, family traditions and adaptation to a challenging environment. Other communities live across the wider Lake Turkana region, each with its own identity and traditions.

### Experiences to consider

Learn about beadwork and traditional craftsmanship, explore local markets, ask about pastoral livelihoods and discover how fishing supports communities along the lake.

Where possible, arrange visits through local guides or community-led tourism initiatives. These arrangements can provide more meaningful experiences while ensuring that local hosts benefit from visitors.

The Lake Turkana Cultural Festival, associated with Loiyangalani, is a notable celebration of the region's cultural diversity. If you hope to attend, confirm the official dates before planning your travel.

Always ask permission before taking photographs and follow local guidance about appropriate behaviour at cultural sites and community gatherings.

## 11. Wildlife and Birdwatching

The Lake Turkana region supports a variety of wildlife and bird species, making it interesting for nature enthusiasts.

The lake is an important habitat for Nile crocodiles and numerous birds, while the surrounding landscapes support wildlife adapted to arid conditions.

Bring binoculars if birdwatching is a priority. A camera with a telephoto lens can also help you photograph wildlife without approaching it.

The most rewarding wildlife experiences often involve patience. Spend time observing your surroundings rather than expecting animals to appear on demand.

Follow the advice of guides and park staff, keep a safe distance from wildlife and never attempt to feed or touch wild animals.

## 12. Photography and Stargazing

Turkana's open landscapes offer excellent opportunities for photography.

The lake's colour, volcanic hills, rocky terrain and expansive skies create striking compositions, especially during early morning and late afternoon.

If you're interested in photography, consider carrying a wide-angle lens for landscapes, spare batteries, additional memory cards and a tripod for low-light images.

The region can also offer memorable stargazing experiences away from bright artificial lights. On a clear night, the open sky provides a wonderful opportunity to appreciate the stars.

Choose a safe location with your host or guide, check the weather and avoid wandering away from your accommodation alone after dark.

## 13. Where to Stay When Exploring Turkana

Your choice of accommodation should reflect the destinations you intend to visit.

Lodwar is a practical base for visitors who want access to town facilities and need to organise excursions before travelling farther afield. Eliye Springs is worth considering if you want to spend more time by the lake, while Loiyangalani is better suited to travellers exploring the southeastern region.

For remote parks and islands, check whether specialist camps or other suitable accommodation are available along your planned route.

If you're flying into Lodwar, staying in town before beginning your journey can make it easier to organise supplies, rest after travelling and confirm your transport.

Luxora Hotel Lodwar is located next to Lodwar Airport and is one option for visitors looking for accommodation in town. As with any property, confirm current availability, facilities and arrangements before booking.

The most important consideration is choosing a base that fits your itinerary rather than attempting to cover distant attractions from a single location.

## 14. Suggested Itineraries

### A short 2 to 3-day visit

**Day 1:** Arrive in Lodwar, settle into your accommodation and explore the town.

**Day 2:** Travel towards Eliye Springs, enjoy the lakeside scenery and return to Lodwar or stay overnight near the lake.

**Day 3:** Enjoy a relaxed morning, explore a locally arranged cultural experience if available, and depart.

This option works best for travellers with limited time who want to experience the western side of the lake.

### A 4 to 5-day western-shore trip

**Day 1:** Arrive in Lodwar and confirm your transport and excursion arrangements.

**Day 2:** Explore Eliye Springs and spend time by the lake.

**Day 3:** Visit the Kalokol area and learn about the fishing economy or arrange a suitable lake excursion.

**Day 4:** Choose an additional activity based on local availability, such as photography, cultural exploration or a more relaxed lakeside visit.

**Day 5:** Return to Lodwar and depart.

### A longer northern Kenya expedition

For a longer trip, consider exploring the western shore before undertaking a separately organised journey towards Loiyangalani or Sibiloi.

Allow several additional days for remote travel, and confirm the road conditions, accommodation, vehicle requirements and other logistics before committing to the itinerary.

Do not assume that destinations on opposite sides of Lake Turkana can be connected quickly. The distances and terrain make realistic planning essential.

## 15. Practical Advice for First-Time Visitors

Before leaving for any excursion, confirm transport, accommodation, park requirements and expected journey times.

Carry sufficient drinking water, sun protection and food for the duration of the trip. Save offline maps and important contacts, and keep cash available where electronic payment facilities may be limited.

Ask local guides about current road and security conditions, particularly if you intend to visit remote destinations.

Respect local customs, seek permission before photographing people and avoid leaving litter behind.

Most importantly, do not try to fit every attraction into a short stay. Turkana is best appreciated when you have time to enjoy the landscape and understand the places you visit.

## Final Thoughts: Give Turkana Time

Turkana offers much more than a view of Lake Turkana. Its appeal lies in the combination of extraordinary scenery, archaeological discoveries, wildlife and cultural heritage.

For first-time visitors, Lodwar provides a practical starting point for organising the journey. From there, you can explore the western lakeshore, arrange local cultural experiences or plan a more ambitious expedition into the remote parts of northern Kenya.

Start with the destinations that interest you most, allow enough time for the distances involved and seek local guidance when planning unfamiliar routes.

With thoughtful preparation, your visit can become an opportunity to discover a remarkable part of Kenya at a pace that allows you to appreciate its landscapes, people and history.
